Our Story

Rooted in beauty,
grown with love.

Bloom & Petal started in 2014 as a small corner flower stand. Today, we're a neighborhood studio dedicated to bringing joy through thoughtfully designed bouquets — one stem at a time.

Every arrangement is made to order by hand, using the freshest blooms we can find. We believe flowers are more than decoration; they're a way to say what words can't.
